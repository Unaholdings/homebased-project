Frequently asked questions

Can you make a key if I've lost all of them?

Yes. We decode the lock, cut a new key and program it to your vehicle on-site. Have your ID and proof of ownership (registration papers) ready.

Why do you need proof of ownership?

It protects you and us. We only cut vehicle keys for verified owners, and for homes we confirm you live at the address.

Will the price change when you arrive?

You get a confirmed quote on WhatsApp once we know your vehicle or lock type, and before work starts.

How does the location pin work?

With your permission, your browser shares GPS coordinates that we turn into a Google Maps link inside the WhatsApp message. Nothing is stored on this website. You can type your address instead.
